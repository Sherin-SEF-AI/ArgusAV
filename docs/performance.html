Performance

What it costs, how well it works

Every number on this page was measured on the rig (a Jetson AGX Orin 64 GB, 12 CPU cores, on the bench with the deserializer's internal trigger and no GNSS fix), or — where marked — on simulated data with exact truth. Nothing is estimated.

Real-data checks

  • Camera-moved check on a real scene: 0.0 px on all six unchanged views; a simulated 6 px bump reported at 5.98 px.
  • Search by meaning on recorded office footage: text queries for people standing found them; black frames no longer match every query.
  • 3D pre-labels on real footage with nominal geometry: 11 people found and tracked in a 61 s session.
  • GNSS interference on this receiver: MON-RF (indicator, antenna, AGC) and NAV-STATUS (spoofing) work; the jamming state is not in this firmware.
